WMOOP — Brand & Logo System

The world as an object graph · one mark, many languages

1 · Scientific concept — academic / community emblem

A seal for papers, slides and the community hub. Centre = the concept mark; the ring carries the full name.

2 · The concept mark

Wire globe = the world. Nodes = objects/classes in a graph. The amber node is a role attached to the whole by a composition diamond — the project's thesis, drawn in.

3 · Organisation lockup

wmoop-logo-light.svg / wmoop-logo-dark.svg, or wmoop-logo.svg which flips with the OS theme.

4 · Per-repo pattern (language icon in the mark)

The globe + composition/role motif is constant. Each repo adds a language-coloured orbit ring, a corner chip with the language glyph, and a coloured .ext.

The template, for new bindings.
